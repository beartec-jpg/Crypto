Additional Helpful Indicators for Graded Trade Ideas
Beyond the core list (RSI, MACD, CCI, Stochastic, ADX/+DI/-DI, CVD, Volume Profile, Order Blocks/FVGs/Absorption, OI/Funding/Long-Short), these indicators add confluence for SMC/ICT, short-term crypto trading, momentum, volatility, and order flow. They improve grading by confirming structure, divergences, trend strength, or institutional activity.
Momentum & Oscillators
Stochastic Oscillator (14,3,3 or faster like 5,3,3) — Detects overbought/oversold in ranging markets; crossovers for timing entries. Complements RSI (less choppy in ranges) and spots hidden divergences.
Money Flow Index (MFI) (14-period) — Volume-weighted RSI; highlights divergences with real buying/selling pressure. Useful for confirming CVD trends.
Chaikin Money Flow (CMF) — Measures accumulation/distribution; positive/negative flow for bias confirmation.
Ultimate Oscillator or Williams %R — Multi-timeframe momentum; better for short-term reversals in volatile crypto.
Volatility & Trend
Average True Range (ATR) (14-period) → Sizes SL/TP dynamically (e.g., 1-2x ATR); identifies displacement (strong moves) vs. fakeouts (low ATR).
Bollinger Bands (20,2) → Squeeze for breakout potential; bandwidth for volatility regime. Pairs with ADX (low bandwidth + low ADX = range).
VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) → Institutional benchmark; deviations show premium/discount zones. Anchored VWAP for session resets.
Order Flow & Volume Enhancements
On-Balance Volume (OBV) — Trend confirmation via volume; divergences signal exhaustion.
Volume Oscillator or Volume Spike Detection — Short vs. long-term volume pressure; confirms absorption/exhaustion.
Footprint/Cluster Data (if available) — Bid/Ask imbalance per bar; stacked imbalances for hidden order flow.
SMC/ICT-Specific
Break of Structure (BOS)/Change of Character (CHoCH) — Auto-detection of market structure shifts; core for directional bias.
Displacement (price thrust with volume) — Confirms real moves (e.g., via ATR bands or candle size).
These prioritize confluence without overload—e.g., add Stochastic + ATR for better short-term timing. For crypto specifics (perps), Funding Rate bias and OI delta remain key. Provide a symbol/timeframe with any of these for a sample graded trade!
